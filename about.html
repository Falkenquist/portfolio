About

How I work

If you understand people, you understand marketing.

My bachelor’s from SDU is in Market and Management Anthropology, with fieldwork in Sri Lanka. In practice that means asking a lot of questions early, whether it’s about members in a small Danish town or engineers explaining a loudspeaker array.

I spent six years working for international companies in Berlin, and much of my work since has been the step between a global plan and the local version of it. I like the combination of creativity and numbers, and in marketing I think they belong together.

I’m happiest in small teams where I’m close to the decisions and can see the team succeed together. I have loved technology since I was a kid, I use AI tools every day, and I like finding smarter ways to work. I also grew up in a home full of paper and print, since my dad is a typographer, and I have made almost every kind of printed material there is.

Outside work I’m usually around live music. I volunteer at the venues Posten and Dexter in Odense, have been a volunteer editor at the music magazine Lyd i Odense and have volunteered at Roskilde, Tinderbox, Heartland and Northside. At UEFA EURO 2024 I volunteered as Media Operations Manager.

Danish (native), English (fluent), German (B2). I also understand Swedish and Norwegian better than most Danes.
